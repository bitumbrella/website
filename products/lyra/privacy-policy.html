Lyra Privacy policy

Effective December 4, 2026

Lyra is an app by Bit Umbrella ("we", "us") that helps you capture, describe and find everyday things in your life - documents, receipts, everyday items - from a photo or a document. This policy explains what information Lyra collects, how it's used and shared, and the choices you have.

In short
  • We collect what you give Lyra - photos, documents, notes, voice notes, questions - to provide its features, and nothing more.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Photos, documents and voice are processed by trusted cloud services (listed below) to analyse them and answer your questions.
  • You can delete any Asset at any time, or delete all your data in the app or at Delete my data.

1. Information we collect

Your Google sign-in

You sign in to Lyra with Google. Lyra doesn't create a separate account or password. From Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. Your email address is stored with your data to identify it as yours; your name and picture are only shown in the app and aren't stored on our servers.

Photos and what we learn from them

  • Photos you take or pick to create an Asset, stored in the cloud so they're available on your devices.
  • Details extracted from each photo: a short caption, descriptive tags, dominant colours, text printed in the image (OCR), and numerical representations of the image and text ("embeddings") used to find similar items.
  • Before a photo is stored, the information a camera writes into the file (including any GPS location) is removed, on your phone and again on our servers.
  • Photos you scan to search your library are analysed to find matches and are not stored.

Documents and what we learn from them

  • Documents you choose to add to an Asset - PDF, Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx) files, such as an insurance policy or a receipt - stored in the cloud as you uploaded them, with their file name, size and number of pages.
  • An image of the document: its first page for a PDF, or a generic Word or Excel icon, shown as its picture in the app.
  • Details extracted from each document: the text of every page, the kind of document (for example "Motor insurance policy") and labelled details such as a policy number, dates or amounts, taken from its first pages. A PDF's first page also gets an image embedding, so a scan of the paper copy can find it.
  • A document's own text is read on our servers. A scanned document (a PDF of page images) is sent whole to a text recognition service to read its text; a PDF's first-page image is sent to create its embedding; the text of its first pages (not the file) is sent to extract the labelled details; and its text is sent, in short passages, to create text embeddings so you can search it. Excel files' labelled details come from their rows, on our servers. See section 3 for these services.
  • Lyra refuses Word and Excel files with macros, password-protected files, and older .doc and .xls files, and doesn't run anything in a document.

Notes and voice

  • Text notes you add to an Asset, and any text you edit.
  • Voice notes (audio recordings up to 10 seconds) you record for an Asset, and their text transcriptions.
  • Ask Lyra: when you ask a question by voice, the recording is sent to be transcribed and is not stored. Your question and Lyra's answer are processed to produce the answer (and to read it aloud) and are not stored, unless you report the answer (see "Feedback and reports").

Location

With your permission, when you create an Asset with the camera, Lyra records the precise location where the photo was taken and looks up its street address, so you can later find things by place. Lyra only uses location while you're using the app, never in the background. If you decline, Assets are created without a location. Photos picked from your gallery are not given a location.

Feedback and reports

  • Feedback you send: its type, an optional rating, your message, an optional screenshot, and your email.
  • Reported AI answers: if you report an Ask Lyra answer, we receive your question, the answer, the reason you chose, any comment you add, and references to the Assets the answer was based on - so we can review it.

Information kept on your device

The Android app keeps some information on your phone: your Google sign-in token, your settings (such as your data region, whether to keep photos on this phone, which extracted details to show, and your answers to Lyra's permission questions), your recent searches, cached images, and links to photos in your gallery. If you choose Keep photos on this phone, photos you take are saved to a "Lyra" album in your phone's gallery. A document you download is saved to your phone's Downloads/Lyra folder; one you open is copied to the app's cache and opened in an app on your phone that you choose. This information stays on your device.

Technical information

Our servers keep operational logs (request timestamps, HTTP methods, IP addresses, routing paths, and server performance metrics) and error details - to keep Lyra secure and working. They don't include the content of your photos, documents, notes, voice notes, searches or questions.

Usage counts

We count how much you use Lyra's features: how many photos and document pages you add, how many questions you ask, how many seconds of voice are transcribed, how many characters are read aloud, and how many scans you run, each with its date. We use these counts to apply your plan's monthly allowance and to understand what Lyra costs to run. We also count, per day, how many searches and questions you run and how many of them found nothing, and how often you reach one of Lyra's limits (your plan's allowance, too many requests in a minute, a file or recording that's too large). We keep your plan, when you first and last used Lyra, and the version of the app you last used. We use these to see whether search works well, whether a limit is too tight, and who needs to update the app. None of this includes the content of your photos, documents, notes, voice notes, searches or questions.

What we don't collect

Lyra doesn't access your contacts, messages or other apps' data, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Lyra only reads photos you explicitly choose (through Android's photo picker) or take with it, and documents you explicitly choose (through Android's file picker).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide Lyra's features: storing and showing your Assets, describing photos, reading documents, finding information by scan and location, transcribing voice, answering your questions, and reading answers aloud.
  • To store your data in the region you chose and keep it available across your devices.
  • To review feedback and reported AI answers and improve Lyra.
  • To keep Lyra secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To apply your plan's allowances and understand what Lyra costs to run (usage counts).

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or use your photos or notes to build profiles about you.

4. Where your information is stored

When you first sign in to the Lyra app, you choose where your data is stored - Asia or rest of the world - and that choice can't be changed from the app later. What Lyra stores about you is kept in that region:

Asia Rest of the world
Database (Northflank) Southeast Asia Central United States
Photos, documents and voice notes (Amazon S3) Singapore Ohio, United States
Photo analysis (Azure AI Vision) Southeast Asia Central United States

Some processing happens in the United States whichever region you choose: describing photos, reading documents and the text in photos, transcribing voice, creating text embeddings and answering your questions run on Microsoft Azure services (Azure OpenAI, Speech and Content Understanding) in the South Central United States. Your information is sent there to be processed, and what comes back is stored in your chosen region.

Lemonfox, which reads Ask Lyra's answers aloud, runs its service on servers in several countries, so an answer may be processed outside your region. Lemonfox doesn't keep what it receives: the text of an answer is deleted as soon as its audio has been created.

5. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • Assets (photos, documents, details, notes, voice notes, transcriptions and locations) are kept until you delete them. Deleting an Asset, or removing a photo or document from it, removes its stored files.
  • Delete my data: in the Android app (profile menu → Delete my data) or at bitumbrella.tech/lyra/delete-my-data.html, you can permanently delete all your Assets and their files on every Lyra server, and any AI answers you've reported. Deletion is immediate.
  • Feedback, including any screenshot you attached, is kept to help us improve Lyra. When you delete your data, your email is removed from it.
  • Usage counts are kept when you delete your data, so that deleting doesn't reset your monthly allowance. Your email is removed from them; they keep only a one-way code made from it (a hash), which lets us match them to you if you sign in again, and no content.
  • Server logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted.
  • Backups: our databases are backed up daily and each backup is kept for 7 days. If you delete your data, it will be gone from backups within 7 days.
  • On your device: data kept by the Android app is removed when you use Delete my data, clear the app's data, or uninstall it. Photos saved to your gallery and documents saved to Downloads stay there - they're yours.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See your Assets, photos and documents in the app at any time, and edit their notes. Extracted tags, text and colours aren't shown unless you turn them on in the profile menu, and extracted text can't be edited.
  • Delete individual Assets, or all your data, as described above.
  • Permissions: microphone (voice notes and Ask Lyra) and location (camera captures) are only requested when needed and can be turned off in your phone's settings. Lyra keeps working without them.
  • Keep photos on this phone can be switched on or off from the profile menu.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your personal information, such as to access, correct, or obtain a copy of it, or to object to its processing. 7. Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every request to our servers must carry a valid Google sign-in that we verify, and your Assets, photos and voice notes are only ever made available to you. Stored files aren't public: the links used to show or download them are temporary and only issued to their owner. A small number of Bit Umbrella team members can see account emails, usage counts, feedback and reported answers, to run and support Lyra. Our admin tools don't show your Assets, photos, documents or notes. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Lyra is a general-audience app. We don't ask for your age, and we don't design or market Lyra for children. If you're under the age at which you can agree to the use of your data where you live (for example 18 in India, 13 in the United States, and between 13 and 16 in the UK and EU), please use Lyra only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has used Lyra without your permission, contact us and we'll delete their data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Lyra changes. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
