{{ site.app_name }}

Extracted Knowledge Organizer & Voice Assistant

Your photos and documents, finally easy to find.

Photos pile up, and important documents get lost in folders and inboxes. {{ site.app_name }} is a smart library for both. Add a photo or a document once, and {{ site.app_name }} reads it, describes it and remembers it - so later you can find it, or simply ask about it, in your own words.

Smart photo library

Photos that know what's in them

Every photo you add to {{ site.app_name }} becomes an Asset with real context around it. {{ site.app_name }} describes what's in the picture, tags it, notes its main colours, reads any text in it, and - if you allow it - remembers where you took it. Add your own notes, or record a quick voice note and {{ site.app_name }} writes it down for you.

Group up to 20 photos in one Asset - the front and back of a warranty card, every angle of something you're selling - and keep them on your phone as well as safely in the cloud.

Smart document library

Upload a document once. Find anything in it in seconds.

Add PDFs, Word and Excel files - insurance policies, receipts, bills, contracts, statements. {{ site.app_name }} reads every page, works out what kind of document it is, and picks out the details that matter, such as policy numbers, dates and amounts. Even a scanned PDF or a photo of a paper document is read.

No more opening file after file to find one number: search or ask, and {{ site.app_name }} takes you straight to the right document, with the original one tap away.

Ask {{ site.app_name }}

Ask a question. Get the answer, not a list of files.

Type or simply hold the mic and ask. {{ site.app_name }} answers instantly, using only what's in your own library - and only what's relevant to your question - and can read the answer aloud. Under every answer you see the Assets it came from, so the original photo or document is always one tap away to check.

  • When does my car insurance expire?
  • What's the policy number on my health insurance?
  • How much did I pay for the fridge?
  • Where did I park at the airport?

Smart search

Describe it the way you remember it

You don't need the exact words. Describe what you're looking for in a full sentence, and {{ site.app_name }} understands what you mean - not just the words you typed. Prefer keywords? Those work too, and you can narrow results by tag, colour or place.

  • the blue jacket I bought last winter
  • receipt for the new laptop
  • handwritten recipe from grandma
  • passport

Or just point your camera: Scan a thing in front of you and {{ site.app_name }} finds it in your library.
