Use this page to permanently delete everything {{ site.app_name }} stores about you - no app needed.

What gets deleted

  • All your Assets, with their notes, voice-note transcriptions, tags, colours, extracted text and locations
  • Their photos, documents and voice notes stored in the cloud
  • {{ site.app_name }} answers you've reported to us (they include your question)

Deletion is immediate and covers every {{ site.app_name }} server (Asia and rest of the world). It can't be undone.

What's kept

  • Feedback you've sent us, with your email address removed
  • Server logs, which may include your email address, until they're deleted under our routine log retention

{{ site.app_name }} doesn't create an account - you sign in with Google, and your Google account isn't affected. Photos in your phone's gallery aren't touched. Data kept on your phone by the {{ site.app_name }} app is removed when you use "Delete my data" in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall it.
